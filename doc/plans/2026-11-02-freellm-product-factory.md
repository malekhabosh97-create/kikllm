# FreeLLM Work: product delivery and reusable teams

Date: 2026-11-02

## Outcome

Preserve Paperclip companies, governance, issues, connectors and skills. Browser
chat is the inference transport. A project director owns a persisted delivery
contract; specialists produce isolated deliveries; acceptance and independent
review govern completion. Provider failures preserve evidence and do not replay
unknown actions.

## Implementation

1. Reuse the native team catalog and company lifecycle for reusable departments.
2. Start products in company-scoped independent folders and native task runs.
3. Persist typed plans, dependencies, ownership and acceptance evidence.
4. Isolate delegated work; stage integration with conflict and approval checks.
5. Expose assigned connector tools and company skills through native policies.
6. Show plans, product tasks, capabilities and previews in the existing workbench.
7. Verify realistic local workflows, then a live browser run if the account allows it.

## Evidence and limits

Research decisions and primary links are recorded in the companion capability,
workspace and delivery-contract notes. No new inference subscriptions are
required. Logical company isolation is distinct from an OS execution sandbox.
No claim of fifty concurrent browser sessions or universally reliable website
automation is made. Tests must distinguish simulated model transport from live
browser/model evidence.
